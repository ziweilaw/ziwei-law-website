Three things other readers get wrong

🎯

Read exactly what you choose

Textbooks are long. Pick a chapter from the book’s own contents, or type a page range, and it starts there.

  • Chapter & section picker
  • Page ranges for PDFs (PDF or printed numbers)
  • Click or highlight any sentence to continue from it
🤫

Skips the side-notes

No more “open parenthesis Smith comma 2019”. Each of these can be switched on or off:

  • Inline citations: (Smith, 2020) and [1, 3]
  • Footnotes and endnotes
  • Figure captions, reference lists, running headers and page numbers
🎙️

Doesn’t sound robotic

Uses a modern neural voice that runs on your own computer, so your papers never leave it.

  • 7 natural voices (US & UK)
  • Longer pauses at paragraphs and headings
  • Speed 0.7×–2× without chipmunk pitch

How it works

Open a book

Choose a PDF or EPUB. Read4me finds the chapters and spots footnotes, citations and headers.

Choose what to hear

Select a chapter, or enter pages. Tick or untick what to skip.

Listen, and take notes

Follow along as the current sentence is highlighted. Jot notes as you go and download them when you’re done.

Honest limits (it’s an early version)

  • Citation and footnote detection is rule-based. It works well on typical journal articles and ebooks, but unusual layouts can slip through. Every skip can be switched off.
  • Scanned PDFs (pictures of pages) aren’t supported yet; text-based PDFs and EPUBs are.
  • Mac first: I’m preparing it for the Mac App Store. A Windows version depends on how many people ask for it.
  • Copy-protected (DRM) ebooks can’t be opened.
